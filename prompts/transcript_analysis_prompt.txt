# Transcript Analysis Prompt

You are analyzing a conversation transcript to create an expert avatar agent. Your primary task is to extract and structure information that will be used to build an AI agent that emulates the user, allowing others to have conversations with their avatar about their expertise.

## Primary Goal: Create Avatar Agent Content

The most important output is the **Conversation Narrative**, which will serve as the foundational knowledge base for an avatar agent. This agent will represent the user in conversations with other people, answering questions and discussing topics within the user's area of expertise. The narrative must be comprehensive and capture the user's voice, perspectives, and knowledge in detail.

## Your Task

Review the conversation transcript and metadata provided below, and extract the following information:

### Core Content (Primary Purpose: Avatar Agent Creation)

1. **Metadata**: Extract session_id, user_id, user_firstname, user_lastname, and created_at from the provided metadata.

2. **Conversation Narrative**: This is the PRIMARY output that will be used to create the avatar agent. Create a detailed, thorough, and comprehensive summary of the entire conversation that captures:
   - The user's expertise, knowledge, and perspectives
   - Their communication style, tone, and personality
   - Specific examples, stories, and experiences they shared
   - Their viewpoints, opinions, and reasoning
   - Direct quotes from the user that exemplify their voice and thinking
   
   Focus on thoroughness and depth rather than brevity - this content will teach the avatar agent how to think and speak like the user. Always refer to the user by their actual name (found in the metadata) rather than as "user" or "the participant."

### Avatar Personality & Character (Purpose: Avatar Behavior & Authenticity)

3. **Myers-Briggs Code**: Assess the user's personality based on the conversation and assign a Myers-Briggs personality type code (e.g., INTJ, ENFP, etc.). Analyze their communication style, thinking patterns, energy orientation, and decision-making approach to determine the most fitting type. This will guide the avatar's personality and interaction style.

4. **Personality Description**: Provide a detailed description of the user's personality as observed in the conversation, written in natural, everyday language as if you're describing them to another person. DO NOT use Myers-Briggs terminology or jargon. Include:
   - Communication style and mannerisms
   - Energy and enthusiasm levels
   - Approach to explaining concepts
   - Social interaction patterns
   - Thinking and problem-solving style
   - Any distinctive personality traits or quirks
   
   **CRITICAL**: NEVER describe the user as someone who "pauses to think," "takes time to reflect," or any similar phrasing that suggests deliberate slowness. Latency is critical in voice AI, and we do not want to encourage the avatar to be slower than necessary. Focus on the content and style of their communication, not their pacing.
   
   This detailed personality profile will be used to ensure the avatar behaves authentically like the user.

5. **Expertise Description**: Write a succinct description of the user's area of expertise and background. This should be 2-4 sentences that capture:
   - Their primary domain of expertise
   - Key professional background or credentials
   - Current work or focus area
   - What makes them uniquely qualified in their field

### Supplementary Content (Ancillary Purpose: Promotion & Marketing)

6. **Main Topic**: Identify the primary topic or subject that the user discussed in the conversation. This should be a concise phrase capturing the core theme. This will be used for categorization and discovery. It should be five words or less.

7. **Hot Takes**: Extract TWO compelling statements from the transcript that represent the user's strongest or most interesting viewpoints. These "hot takes" should:
   - Use the same voice, tone, and word choice as the user
   - Be grammatically perfect and well-articulated (clean up any verbal stumbles or filler words)
   - Be suitable for use in a short avatar video (typically 1-2 sentences each)
   - Capture the user's authentic perspective and personality
   - These will be used to create promotional avatar video clips

8. **Social Post Draft**: Write engaging social media post copy (approximately 2 sentences) that will accompany the hot take avatar videos and promote the avatar agent. Structure it as:
   - First sentence: A thesis statement that introduces the post content
   - Second sentence: A call-to-action (CTA) inviting people to engage with the avatar
   - Example: "Evals are the hardest part of building software. Talk to my expert avatar to learn why."
