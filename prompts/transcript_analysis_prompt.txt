# Transcript Analysis Prompt

You are analyzing a conversation transcript from an onboarding interview. Your task is to extract and structure key information about the user that will be used to create their expert avatar.

## Your Task

Review the following statements from the user and extract structured information about:

1. **Area of Expertise**: What are they an expert in? What's their specific domain or field?

2. **Background & Journey**: How did they develop this expertise? What experiences shaped their knowledge?

3. **Common Misconceptions**: What are TWO things that people new to their field commonly get wrong or didn't expect?

4. **Current Project/Work**: What are they building or working on right now? Be specific about the project details.

5. **Problem They're Solving**: What problem are they solving for their customers or users? What pain point are they addressing?

6. **General Interests**: What topics do they enjoy discussing? What subjects get them engaged in conversation?

## Output Format

Please provide your analysis in the following JSON structure:

```json
{
  "expertise": {
    "domain": "string - their area of expertise",
    "summary": "string - brief description of their expertise"
  },
  "background": {
    "journey": "string - how they developed their expertise",
    "key_experiences": ["array of key experiences that shaped them"]
  },
  "misconceptions": [
    {
      "misconception": "string - first common misconception",
      "reality": "string - what the reality actually is"
    },
    {
      "misconception": "string - second common misconception", 
      "reality": "string - what the reality actually is"
    }
  ],
  "current_work": {
    "project": "string - what they're building",

  },
  "problem_solving": {
    "problem": "string - the problem they're solving",
    "target_audience": "string - who benefits",

}
```
